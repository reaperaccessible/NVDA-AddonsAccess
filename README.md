# NVDA-AddonsAccess

Accessible add-ons for the NVDA screen reader, by ReaperAccessible.
Extensions accessibles pour le lecteur d'écran NVDA, par ReaperAccessible.

## Add-ons

- **LocalTranslateAccess** — translates what NVDA says, on your own computer. French and English are included; other languages are downloaded as language packs. NVDA+Shift+T translates the selected text; NVDA+Ctrl+Shift+T opens the translation menu.
- **ToneTunerAccess** — reference tones to tune a guitar by ear: each string, all the strings or a chord, once or in a loop. NVDA+Ctrl+Shift+A opens the tuner. Based on Reference Tone Tuner by Leandro Souza (GPL v2).
- **ResourceMonitorAccess** — announces the state of the computer: processor, applications using the most, memory, drives, network connection, Internet speed test, Wi-Fi, battery, Windows version, time since Windows started and NVIDIA graphics card; reports Wi-Fi connections and disconnections. NVDA+Ctrl+Shift+M opens the resource window. Based on Resource Monitor by Alex Hall, Joseph Lee, Kefas Lungu and other NVDA contributors (GPL v2).
- **KeyboardAccess** — keyboard tools: gives back the Applications key to keyboards that do not have one, clicks the selected item, and changes the keyboard language among all those of Windows without adding them in its settings. NVDA+Ctrl+Shift+K opens the keyboard window. Based on Remap Applications Key by Rui Fontes, Ângelo Abrantes and Héctor J. Benítez Corredera (GPL v2).
- **AudioManagerAccess** — the sound of Windows in one window: outputs and inputs (volume, mute, default device), each application with its own volume and its own output and input, NVDA's output and a lock for the microphone volume. NVDA+Ctrl+Shift+P opens the sound window. Based on Audio Manager by huaiyinfeilong (GPL v2).
- **WindowsToolsAccess** — Windows maintenance tools in one window with six tabs: quick tools (Internet, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi password, USB drive…), cleaning, programs started with Windows, services, Windows panels and advanced tools (DISM, SFC, Chkdsk, Defender…). NVDA+Ctrl+Shift+W opens the window. Based on AbsoluteWindows by Chai Chaimee and Peera Pilarit (GPL).
- **AgendaAccess** — an agenda made for NVDA: the appointments of each day, repetitions of every kind (chosen days of the week, the 2nd Tuesday of the month…), reminders spoken by NVDA with ringtones and also sent to the phone with ntfy if wanted, appointments with a place, an end, all day or over several days, a time zone, and more details (importance, status, availability, private, organizer, meeting link), one day of a series changed alone or from a day on, reminders at a chosen delay or time and "remind me again later", a search, a journal, an agenda shared between computers with Dropbox or OneDrive, several agendas, someone else's agenda shown with yours, online agendas, and .ics import and export for Outlook, Google and others. NVDA+Ctrl+Shift+Enter opens the agenda. Inspired by Agenda by Abel Passos, Ângelo Abrantes and Rui Fontes, and by Mon agenda by Louis-Rock Lampron.
- **BeMyAssistantAccess** — an AI assistant for NVDA: describes the screen, an object or a photo, reads documents and images, translates, explains videos, answers questions in a chat, and guides you step by step to click or type in an application, with each action confirmed by you. Works with a local AI on your own computer (installed with the ReaperAccessible Installer Manager) or with an online service such as Google Gemini. NVDA+Ctrl+Shift+V opens the commands. Based on Vision Assistant Pro by Mahmood Hozhabri.
- **SpeakWriteAccess** — corrects the text you select, writes what you say into the microphone and transcribes your audio and video files, with an AI that runs on your own computer (installed with the ReaperAccessible Installer Manager): nothing is sent over the Internet. NVDA+Ctrl+Shift+O corrects the selection; NVDA+Ctrl+Shift+L starts or stops the dictation, written phrase by phrase or all at once, with the microphone of your choice, and even translated into another language; NVDA+Ctrl+Shift+F transcribes an audio or video file; it can also add emojis to the selected text (no key at first); NVDA+Ctrl+Shift+E says what it is doing.
- **SpeechHistoryAccess** — review and copy NVDA's speech history. Interface in French when NVDA is in French, in English otherwise. Based on Speech History by Tyler Spivey and James Scholes (GPL v2).

## Installation

Download the `.nvda-addon` file from the [Releases](https://github.com/reaperaccessible/NVDA-AddonsAccess/releases) page and open it; NVDA offers to install it.

Each add-on then checks this repository for new versions when NVDA starts, and offers to install them.

Téléchargez le fichier `.nvda-addon` depuis la page [Releases](https://github.com/reaperaccessible/NVDA-AddonsAccess/releases) et ouvrez-le ; NVDA propose de l'installer. Chaque extension vérifie ensuite ce dépôt au démarrage de NVDA et propose les nouvelles versions.
