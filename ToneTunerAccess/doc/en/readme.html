ToneTunerAccess

ToneTunerAccess helps you tune a guitar by ear. It plays the reference sound of each string, recorded on a real guitar: you play the same string on your instrument, and turn the tuning peg until you hear the same note.

The add-on does not detect the note you play: your ear does the comparing. It covers the six-string guitar in standard tuning, from the 6th to the 1st string: low E, A, D, G, B, high E.

The add-on is displayed in French when NVDA is in French, and in English in every other language.

Opening the tuner

  • With the NVDA+Ctrl+Shift+A shortcut.
  • Or from the NVDA menu, Tools, ToneTunerAccess.

To change the shortcut, open the NVDA menu, Preferences, Input gestures, "ToneTunerAccess" category.

The keys of the window

  • 1 to 6: play a string, from 1 for the high E to 6 for the low E. The numeric keypad digits work too.
  • T: play all the strings, from the 6th to the 1st, one second apart.
  • S: play the G major chord.
  • M: play the E major chord.
  • R: repeat the sound in a loop, or stop the loop. This is also the "Repeat in a loop" check box of the window.
  • A: lengthen the pause between two repetitions of the loop.
  • D: shorten this pause.
  • F1: show the list of these keys.
  • Escape: stop the sound. If nothing is playing, Escape closes the window.

When a string or a chord starts, NVDA briefly says its name, for example "low E". During a loop, only the first pass is announced, so as not to cover the sound. You can turn this announcement off in the settings.

The pause of the loop goes from 1 to 10 seconds, in quarters of a second. It is remembered for the next time the tuner opens.

Tuning step by step

  1. Open the tuner, then press R to turn the loop on.
  2. Press the digit of the string to tune, for example 6 for the low E. The sound repeats.
  3. Play the same string on your guitar and turn the peg until both sounds merge. When they are close, you often hear a beat, like a slow wobble: it slows down as you get closer, and disappears when the string is in tune.
  4. Go on to the next string with its digit, then press Escape when you are done.

To check everything, press T to hear all the strings, or S and M to compare a chord.

Settings

Open the NVDA menu, Preferences, Settings, then the "ToneTunerAccess" category.

  • Announce the name of the string played: checked by default.
  • Turn the loop on when the tuner opens: unchecked by default. Check it if you always tune with the loop.
  • Pause between two repetitions of the loop: 2 seconds by default. The A and D keys of the window change this setting too.
  • Check for updates automatically when NVDA starts: checked by default.
  • Check for updates now: starts the check right away.

Updates

ToneTunerAccess does not go through NVDA's Add-on Store. The add-on checks by itself, shortly after NVDA starts, whether a new version has been published, and offers to install it. When several ReaperAccessible add-ons are updated together, their windows open one after the other and NVDA asks to restart only once.

In the Add-on Store, the add-on is listed with the "External" channel. This is normal for an add-on installed from outside the Store.

Privacy

The tuner works without a connection. The add-on only contacts the Internet to check for updates, with GitHub.
