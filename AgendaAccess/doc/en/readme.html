AgendaAccess

AgendaAccess is an agenda made for NVDA. You write down your appointments, which can repeat in many ways, and NVDA reminds you of them in advance. Everything is done with the keyboard, and each day can be heard at once. Your agenda can also be shared between several computers, for example with Dropbox.

The add-on is in French when NVDA is in French, and in English in every other language.

Opening the agenda

NVDA+Ctrl+Shift+Enter opens the agenda on today. If the window is already open, the shortcut brings it back to the front.

The agenda window

The window shows the list of the appointments of one day, in order of time. Each line gives the date, the time, the description, the repetition, the reminders, the category and whether there is a note, for example "Wednesday, December 9, 2026, 13:30, Dentist, reminder 15 minutes before, Health, with a note". A day without appointments shows one line, for example "Saturday, November 21, 2026, no appointment". The date is also in the title of the window.

When you change the day, NVDA says the date and the number of appointments, for example "Tomorrow, Friday, November 20, 2026: 2 appointments". The up and down arrows then read them one by one. On the first or last line, or when there is only one appointment, they say the line again.

Moving in time

  • Left and right arrows: the day before or after.
  • Control with the left and right arrows: the week before or after.
  • Page up and page down: the month before or after.
  • Control with page up and page down: the year before or after.
  • J and Shift+J: the next or previous day with appointments.
  • S and Shift+S: week after week, on the same day of the week, up to a day with appointments.
  • Space: the next free day, without appointments.
  • Control+Home: back to today.
  • Control+G: go to a date.

Acting on the appointments

  • Enter: edit the chosen appointment, or create one on an empty day.
  • Control+N: new appointment on the day shown.
  • N: read the note of the chosen appointment.
  • O: open the meeting link of the chosen appointment, if it has one.
  • Delete: delete the chosen appointment.
  • Shift+Delete: delete every appointment of the day, after a confirmation. The ones that repeat keep their other days.
  • Control+U: the upcoming appointments.
  • Control+L: all the appointments, with a search.
  • Control+J: the journal of past appointments.
  • Control+P: pause the reminders.
  • F1: the list of these keys.
  • Escape: close the window.

Below the list, the buttons New appointment, Edit, Delete, Go to a date, Upcoming appointments, More actions, Help and Close do the same. The "More actions" button opens a menu: all the appointments, the journal, the pause of the reminders, the text report, export, import, and assigning keys to the commands.

Creating or editing an appointment

Everything is set in one window. The fields that do not apply to the chosen repetition are greyed out, and Tab skips them.

  • What: the description of the appointment, for example "Dentist". It is required.
  • Agenda: only if you have several agendas of your own (see "Several agendas"): the one the appointment goes to. Your agenda is chosen by default.
  • Category: optional, for example "Health" or "Work". Type a new one, or choose with the arrows a category already used. For a new appointment, choosing a category already used takes the settings of its last appointment: the time, the repetition, the reminders and the sound. Often only the date is left to change.
  • Location: optional, for example the address. It is said after the description.
  • Day, Month, Year: the date. The day and the year change with the up and down arrows, or by typing the number; the month is chosen in a list. A 31st in a shorter month becomes its last day.
  • All day: check this box for an appointment without a time, such as a birthday or a day off. The time is then greyed out, and this appointment comes first in the list of the day.
  • Hour, Minutes: the time of the appointment, in the same way.
  • With an end: check this box to give the appointment an end, then choose the end day, month and year, and the end hour and minutes. An end on the same day gives for example "13:30 to 14:30". An end on another day makes an appointment of several days: it shows on each day, with for example "continued, until 17:00, day 2 of 3". For an all-day appointment, only the last day counts, for example for holidays from July 3 to 10: "all day, day 2 of 8". If the appointment repeats, each time lasts as long.
  • Repeat:
    • Does not repeat.
    • Every day, or every few days: then set "Every how many days", for example 3 for every 3 days.
    • Monday to Friday.
    • Every week, or every few weeks, on chosen days: set "Every how many weeks", then check with the space bar the days of the week you want, for example Tuesday and Thursday.
    • Every month, on the same date: for example the 15th of every month, or every 3 months with "Every how many months". An appointment on the 31st happens on the last day of the shorter months.
    • Every month, on the same day of the week: for example the 2nd Tuesday of the month, from the date chosen. Check "Count the weeks from the end of the month" for the last or second to last Tuesday.
    • Every year, on the same date: an appointment on February 29 happens on February 28 in the other years.
    • Every year, on the same day of the week: for example the 2nd Monday of October.
  • End of the repetition: Never, On a date (then choose the last day, month and year), or After a number of times (for example 20 for a course held 20 times).
  • Reminders: a list of check boxes, checked with the space bar: at the time of the appointment, 5, 15 or 30 minutes before, 1 or 2 hours before, the day before, 2 or 3 days before, or a week before. You can check several. A new appointment has a reminder 15 minutes before.
  • More reminders: this button opens a window for the reminders that are not in the list. For any delay, set "Delay", for example 45, choose minutes, hours, days or weeks before, then "Add this delay". For a reminder at a time of day, set the days before (0 for the same day, 1 for the day before) and the time, then "Add this time", for example "the day before at 20:00". The list at the top shows these reminders; Delete or "Remove this reminder" takes one away. An all-day appointment has no time: it only has reminders at a time of day, and gets one on the day at 8:00 by default, which you can change here.
  • Sound of its reminders: the usual sound, no sound, one of the ringtones of AgendaAccess, or a WAV or MP3 sound file of your choice, for this appointment only. Each sound is heard as soon as you reach it with the arrows.
  • Note: optional, as long as you want, for example the address or what to bring. The N key of the list reads it.
  • Other details: this button opens a window for what few appointments need:
    • Other categories: besides the main category, check with the space bar the ones that fit, in a ready-made list (Personal, Work, Family, Friends, Medical, Meeting, Birthday, Holidays, Travel, Day off, Sport, Leisure, School, Training, Bank and money, Home, Car, Shopping, Volunteering, Show, Important…) and the ones you already used. "New category" and "Add it" create one.
    • Importance: normal, high or low. An important appointment is said "important", and its reminder starts with "Important".
    • Status: confirmed, tentative (said "to be confirmed") or cancelled. A cancelled appointment stays in the agenda, said "cancelled", but gives no reminder any more.
    • Availability: busy, free, tentative or away. When you add or change a "busy" appointment at the same time as another "busy" one, NVDA warns you: "Careful: at the same time as…".
    • Private: the subject of the appointment is not sent to the phone (it becomes "private appointment" there), nor written in the text report, nor in the public copy of your agenda.
    • Organizer and Meeting link: for example the name of who organizes it, and the address of a Teams or Zoom meeting. The O key of the list opens this link.
    • Time zone: for an appointment whose date and time are those of another country, for example a call with Paris at 16:00, Paris time. Choose the Paris zone; the agenda shows and reminds this appointment at the time of your computer, for example "10:00 (16:00 Brussels, Copenhagen, Madrid, Paris time)", following the summer times of both countries. By default, it is the time of this computer.

The OK button, or Control+Enter from any field, saves the appointment. Escape cancels.

When you edit one day of an appointment that repeats, NVDA asks what you want to change:

  • Only this one: that day alone changes, for example this week's course moved to 15:00. The other days do not move, and this day is said "changed for this day". The repetition cannot be changed here.
  • This one and the following ones: from that day on, the series changes, for example a new room from November. The days before stay as they were.
  • Every one, the whole series: the whole series changes; the date shown is then the day it started. What was changed for one day alone stays changed, except what you change for the whole series that this day had not changed.

From the list of all the appointments (Control+L), editing always changes the whole series.

Deleting an appointment

For a single appointment, NVDA asks you to confirm. For an appointment that repeats, you choose between "Only this day", which keeps the other days, and "Every day", which deletes the whole repetition.

All the appointments

This window, opened with Control+L, shows each appointment once, with its next date, its repetition and its category. An appointment that is over gives its last date.

  • Search: type one or more words; the list keeps only the appointments that contain them, in the description, the note or the category.
  • Category: All, or one category only.
  • Order: by date, the nearest first, or alphabetical.

In the list, Enter edits the appointment and Delete deletes it with all its repetitions, after a confirmation.

The upcoming appointments and the journal

The upcoming appointments (Control+U) are those of the next 30 days, the nearest first. The journal (Control+J) lists the appointments of the last year, the most recent first, with their notes: a record of your activities. In both, Enter shows the day of the appointment in the agenda.

The reminders

When a reminder is due, NVDA says it, for example "Reminder: Dentist, in 15 minutes, at 10:30.", and a short chime plays. The reminder is also shown in a window that stays open until you close it with Enter or Escape, so that it is not missed if you were away from the computer. If other reminders come while it is open, they are added to it.

In this window, "Remind me again in" and the "Remind me again" button give the reminders again later: in 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes, or in 1 hour. The "Dismiss" button, Enter or Escape close the window without giving the reminders again.

The reminders are given by NVDA: the computer must be on and NVDA running. If a reminder falls while NVDA is closed, it is not lost: it is given a few seconds after NVDA next starts, if the appointment is not over yet, with the time left, for example "Reminder: Dentist, in 9 minutes, at 10:30.". If several reminders of one appointment were missed, only the latest is given. A reminder is never given twice, even if you restart NVDA.

If the computer was asleep, a reminder at most 10 minutes late is still given when it wakes up; an older one is not.

Pausing the reminders

Control+P in the agenda, or "Pause the reminders" in the More actions menu, stops every reminder for 30 minutes, 1, 2, 4 or 8 hours, for example during a meeting or a film. The reminders of that time are not given later. During a pause, the same window offers to start the reminders again at once.

Getting the reminders on your phone

AgendaAccess can also send each reminder to your phone, with ntfy (said "notify"), a free app for Android and iPhone. The reminder comes as a notification, read to you by TalkBack or VoiceOver, for example "Reminder: Dentist, in 15 minutes, at 10:30.".

There is no account to create and no password. Your phone subscribes to a secret name, for example "agenda-k3x9p2m7q1wz", a bit like a mailbox: whatever AgendaAccess sends to that name comes to your phone.

Important: the reminders leave from the computer. For them to reach the phone, the computer must be on, not asleep, and NVDA must be running. A reminder that fell while NVDA was closed is sent when NVDA next starts, if the appointment is not over yet.

Step 1: install ntfy on the phone

  1. On Android, open the Play Store; on iPhone, the App Store. Search for "ntfy" and install the free app of that name.
  2. Open the app. If it asks for permission to show notifications, accept: without it, no reminder will show.

Step 2: turn the option on, on the computer

  1. Open the NVDA menu, Preferences, Settings, then the category "AgendaAccess".
  2. Choose the "Advanced options" button.
  3. Check the box "Also send the reminders to my phone, with the ntfy app".
  4. In the field "Secret name to type in the ntfy app", a name is already given. You can keep it. For another name, choose the "Create a new secret name" button: NVDA says the new name, spelling it letter by letter. If you already have a name of your own, clear the field and type yours.
  5. To hear the name letter by letter again, go to the name field and move with the left and right arrows. The name only has lowercase letters without accents, digits and dashes.
  6. Do not close this window yet: you will need it in step 4.

Step 3: subscribe to the secret name on the phone

  1. In the ntfy app, find the button to add a subscription. On Android, it is a "+" button at the bottom right of the screen; on iPhone, a "+" button at the top of the screen. It may also be called "Subscribe to topic" or "Add".
  2. In the "Topic" field, type the secret name exactly as in AgendaAccess: in lowercase, without spaces, with its dashes.
  3. Leave the default server. If there is a "Use another server" box, do not check it.
  4. Confirm with the "Subscribe" button. The subscription then shows in the list of the app.

Step 4: send a test

  1. Back on the computer, in the Advanced options, choose the "Send a test to the phone" button. NVDA says "Sending the test", then "Test sent".
  2. Within a few seconds, your phone should get an "AgendaAccess" notification saying: "Test of AgendaAccess: your reminders will come here.".
  3. If so, confirm the Advanced options with OK, then NVDA's settings with OK. You are done.

If the test does not arrive

  • If NVDA says "The test could not be sent", the computer could not reach the server: check the computer's Internet connection, and that the "Server" field holds https://ntfy.sh.
  • If NVDA says "Test sent" but nothing arrives, check that the secret name is the same, letter for letter, in AgendaAccess and in the ntfy app.
  • Check in the phone's settings that the ntfy app is allowed to show notifications, and that Do not disturb is off.
  • Open the ntfy app, then the subscription: the messages received are there, even if the notification did not ring.
  • Check that the phone is connected to the Internet, by Wi-Fi or mobile data.

Good to know

  • The reminders go to the phone as they are spoken on the computer, once only, even if you chose to repeat the reminders.
  • At night, they still go: your phone's Do not disturb mode decides whether it rings.
  • During a pause of the reminders, nothing is sent to the phone.
  • If your agenda is shared between several computers, check this option on one of them only, or each reminder will come twice.
  • To stop, simply uncheck the box in the Advanced options. You can also unsubscribe in the ntfy app.
  • Keep your secret name to yourself: whoever knows it can get your reminders. If you think it is known, create a new one and subscribe to it on the phone.
  • The text of the reminders goes through the free ntfy.sh server. If you have your own ntfy server, give its address in the "Server" field of the Advanced options, and choose the same server in the app: your reminders will then only go through it.

When NVDA starts

A few seconds after NVDA starts, if there are appointments today, NVDA says them, for example "Today, Thursday, November 19, 2026: 2 appointments: 10:30, Dentist; 14:00, Call". You can turn this off in the settings.

At the same time, the reminders that fell while NVDA was closed are given, if any, as explained in "The reminders". They are also sent to the phone if that option is on.

Several agendas, and someone else's agenda

Besides yours, AgendaAccess can show other agendas, together in the same lists. Each appointment of another agenda is said with its name, for example "agenda Marie". Open the settings of AgendaAccess, then the Agendas button. The list shows your agenda, then the others, with their kind and whether they are shown and give reminders.

  • Add an agenda file: choose an agenda file of AgendaAccess (.json) or an .ics file, then give it a name. For a file of AgendaAccess, NVDA asks whether it is someone else's agenda, that you only read (Yes), or one of your agendas, for example Work or Family, that you can change (No). An .ics file is always read only.
  • Add an online agenda: paste the .ics address that Outlook, Google Calendar or another service gives to share an agenda (it often starts with https:// or webcal://), then give it a name. It is read again every 30 minutes, and stays read only.
  • Rename, Show or hide, Reminders on or off, Remove from the list: for the agenda chosen in the list. Removing an agenda never deletes its file. Your own agenda is always shown, with its reminders; you can only rename it.

An agenda read only is never changed nor written by AgendaAccess: its appointments cannot be changed nor deleted, and give reminders only if you turn them on. Your own agendas besides yours (Work, Family) are changed like yours; when creating an appointment, the "Agenda" field chooses where it goes, and changing it later moves it.

Sharing your agenda with someone

For someone else to see your agenda in their AgendaAccess, check in the Agendas window "Write a public copy of my agenda", and choose where to write it, for example in a Dropbox folder shared with that person. This copy is updated at each change. Your private appointments are only "Busy" there, without subject, location or note, and it has no reminders. The person adds this file as an agenda read only.

Exchanging with Outlook, Thunderbird, Google or the iPhone

The More actions menu of the agenda has:

  • Export to an .ics file: all the appointments shown, with their repetitions, reminders, days removed and days changed alone, in a file these programs can import.
  • Import an .ics file: the appointments of a file exported from one of these programs are added to your agenda. Importing the same file a second time updates the appointments instead of copying them twice. A repetition AgendaAccess cannot read becomes a single appointment, and its note says so.

Sharing your agenda between several computers

Your appointments are kept in one file. By default, it is in NVDA's configuration folder, but you can put it wherever you want, for example in your Dropbox or OneDrive folder, to have the same agenda on all your computers:

  1. On the first computer, open the settings of AgendaAccess and choose the button "Choose where to keep the agenda". Go to your Dropbox folder and confirm. Your appointments are copied there at once.
  2. On each other computer, do the same and choose the agenda file already in your Dropbox folder. Its appointments are kept, and those this computer already had are added to it: nothing is lost.

From then on, a change made on one computer appears on the others as soon as Dropbox has synchronised the file, usually within a minute, without doing anything. Each save joins your changes to the latest version of the file, instead of overwriting it. If Dropbox makes a "conflicted copy" because two computers wrote at the same time, AgendaAccess joins it to your agenda and puts it away with its backups.

Each computer gives the reminders of your shared agenda.

Update AgendaAccess on every computer that shares the agenda. A computer left on an old version (2026.6 or before) that edits an appointment can erase its location, its end and its other reminders. When a computer has an older version than the one that wrote the agenda, AgendaAccess says so when opening the agenda.

Backup, export, import and report

  • Automatic backups: every day, a copy of your agenda is made in the folder agendaAccess-backups of NVDA's configuration folder of this computer. The last 14 are kept. To get one back, use "Import an agenda".
  • Export the agenda: saves a copy of your whole agenda in the file of your choice, for example on a USB drive.
  • Import an agenda: adds to your agenda the appointments of an exported file or of a backup. Those you already have are not copied twice.
  • Save a text report: a text file of your appointments of the next 30 days, 3 months or 12 months, one line per appointment with its note, for example "Thursday, November 19, 2026, 10:30, Dentist". You can print it, or read it on a mobile device.

Commands and shortcuts

Only opening the agenda has a shortcut by default. These commands have none, and you can give them one:

  • New appointment.
  • Announce today's appointments.
  • Announce tomorrow's appointments.
  • Announce the next appointment.
  • Show the upcoming appointments.
  • Show all the appointments.
  • Show the journal of past appointments.
  • Pause the reminders.

To give them a key, choose "Assign keys to the commands" in the More actions menu of the agenda. Choose the command, press Enter, then the key you want: it is assigned at once. If the key is already used, NVDA asks you first. Delete removes the key of a command. You also find them in the NVDA menu, Preferences, Input gestures, category "AgendaAccess".

Settings

Open the NVDA menu, Preferences, Settings, then the category "AgendaAccess".

  • File of the agenda: where the file of your appointments is, and the button Choose where to keep the agenda, described above.
  • Announce today's appointments when NVDA starts: checked by default.
  • Play a sound with the reminders: checked by default. An appointment with its own sound file still plays it.
  • Volume of the reminder sounds: from 10 to 100%, 80% by default. It applies to every sound, ringtones and your own files alike. Each volume is heard as soon as you reach it with the arrows.
  • Also show the reminders in a window that stays until closed: checked by default. Uncheck it to have the reminders only spoken.
  • Advanced options: opens a window with:
    • Usual sound of the reminders: one of the ringtones of AgendaAccess, or a WAV or MP3 sound file of your choice. The ringtones are: Chime (the default), Westminster bells, Bell, Marimba, Harp, Notification, Soft beeps, Digital alarm clock and Gong, then a collection of 35 sounds, each followed by its kind: alarm, wake-up, notification, soft or ringtone, for example "Rush (alarm)". The alarms and wake-up sounds last from 10 seconds to more than a minute. Each sound is heard as soon as you reach it with the arrows, and the "Play it" button plays it again.
    • Give each reminder: once, or 3, 5 or 10 times, a minute apart, until its window is closed.
    • Night mode: between the two hours chosen, 22 and 7 by default, the reminders are spoken and shown, but without sound or repetition.
    • Also send the reminders to my phone, with its secret name and its server: unchecked by default. See "Getting the reminders on your phone".
  • Check for updates automatically when NVDA starts: checked by default.
  • Check for updates now: checks at once.

Updates

AgendaAccess does not go through NVDA's Add-on Store. The add-on itself checks, shortly after NVDA starts, whether a new version is published, and offers to install it. When several ReaperAccessible add-ons are updated together, their windows open one after the other and NVDA asks to restart only once.

In the Add-on Store, the add-on appears with the "External" channel. This is normal for an add-on installed outside the Store.

Your appointments are never in the add-on's folder: they stay after an update, and even after the add-on is removed.

Privacy

Your appointments stay in the file you chose: AgendaAccess sends them nowhere. If you put this file in Dropbox or OneDrive, that service copies it to your other computers. Only the text of the reminders goes to your phone, and only if you turned this option on. Your appointments are not read on Windows' secure screens, such as the sign-in screen. The add-on only contacts the Internet to check for updates, on GitHub, and for the reminders sent to the phone.
